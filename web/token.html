How Ghost Credit works

Four steps, every hour. Each one leaves a record you can check.

  1. 01

    Trades pay a fee

    Every buy or sell of $GHOST on Pons pays 3%: Pons's 1% fee and a 2% creator tax. The 2% tax plus 0.7 points of the Pons fee come to our fee wallet in ETH.

  2. 02

    We claim it hourly

    At the end of each hour our server claims that fee. Each claim is a public transaction.

  3. 03

    25% to holders

    The holder share is split by average balance over 4 readings: at :15, :30, :45 and :60.

  4. 04

    Posted on-chain

    We publish the full list as JSON and post its Merkle root to the GhostLedger contract.

25%to holders, as AI credit
75%to the team wallet. We say this plainly: most of the fee funds the team.

Who gets a share

One simple rule, the same for every wallet.

The rule

Hold an average of at least 100,000 $GHOST over the hour's 4 readings.

# your share for one hour
credit = pool × your average ÷ sum of eligible averages

Rounded down to the micro-dollar. Leftover cents stay in the next hour's pool. Credit is added to your GHOSTKEY balance: sign in with the same wallet to use it.

Not counted

These wallets hold $GHOST but never get a share, so they cannot dilute yours.

    The exact list for each hour is inside that hour's JSON, under excluded.

    • Holding also unlocks the product. A wallet with 100,000+ $GHOST gets 30 free messages a day, 30 free API calls a day and 600 API requests a minute. Sign in with that wallet. Limits
    • Ghost Credit is platform credit for AI, not money. It depends on how much $GHOST is traded. Nothing here promises a price or profit.
    • The 2% creator tax is fixed. Pons locks it when the token launches, so nobody can raise it later.
    • No trades, no credit. An hour without fees is recorded as "no fees" and nothing is posted on-chain.
