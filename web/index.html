        FAQ
        
    Short answers. Honest limits.
    
        
      
    
      
    
        
    Is it really private?
    Privacy here covers who you are, not what you write. The model still reads your question to answer it. Your wallet, IP and device are removed first, and only zero-retention providers are used.
    
        
    What is Zero Data Retention?
    It means the provider keeps nothing after answering. It is the provider's own policy, listed publicly by OpenRouter. OpenRouter still screens for illegal content, as the law requires.
    
        
    Why is it the same price as OpenRouter?
    We charge the exact cost OpenRouter reports for each call. No markup and no top-up fee. Use the Compare button above to check it live.
    
        
    Do I need a wallet to try it?
    No. You get 10 free messages without a wallet. Connect one when you want to top up or use the API.
    
        
    Where is my balance kept?
    Your balance is prepaid AI credit held by the GHOSTKEY server. It is not a token in your wallet. You top up with USDG or ETH on Robinhood Chain, counted 1:1 in US dollars.
    
        
    Why do you keep my wallet address?
    Top-ups and Ghost Credit need it. We keep it so your balance and credit go to the right account. Nothing else about you is stored.
    
        
    What is Ghost Credit?
    Each hour, 25% of the $GHOST creator fee becomes AI credit for holders. It is platform credit for AI, not money. It depends on how much $GHOST is traded, so it can be zero in a quiet hour.
    
        
    Can I check your code?
    Yes. The inspector above shows the exact request we build. The proof page links the source code and every contract.
