API keys

          
Send your key as a Bearer token: Authorization: Bearer gk_live_…. Keys start with gk_live_.

          
We show a key once, when you create it. We keep only a hash and the first characters, so we cannot show it again. Lost a key? Create a new one on your account page.

          
Every key bills the balance of the wallet that made it. A new account gets 10 free calls on low-cost models, so you can test before you top up.

          
Limits per key. On your account page, give any key a name, a daily limit in USD and an expiry date (1 day to 1 year). The limit counts what that key spends from your balance in each UTC day. Requests are sized to fit what is left, parallel calls included, so a key never goes over. At the limit it answers 402 key_daily_limit_reached until 00:00 UTC. After the expiry date it answers 401 key_expired. Your other keys and the chat are not affected.

          
Calls on a key with a limit return what is left today in the X-Ghostkey-Key-Limit-Left header and in ghostkey_receipt.keyDailyLimitLeftUsd.

Models

          
Model ids are the same as on OpenRouter, like openai/gpt-4o-mini or anthropic/claude-sonnet-4. We list only models with at least one Zero Data Retention provider.

          
Prices are OpenRouter's prices, per 1 million tokens. Compare them with OpenRouter from your browser.

        


        

          
Billing

          
Prepaid. You add balance with ETH or USDG on Robinhood Chain. The top-up fee is 0%. One dollar paid is one dollar of credit.

          
Hold, then settle. When a call starts, we hold the most it could cost (your max_tokens at the model's price). When it ends, we charge the exact cost OpenRouter reports for that call and release the rest. If the call fails, the hold is released in full.

          
No markup. The amount billed is the cost OpenRouter reports in usage.cost. Every chat answer shows it, and your usage table lists every call.

          
Receipts. Every response carries generationId (OpenRouter's ID for that answer) and forwardedSha256 (a hash of the exact body we sent, kept only in your receipt). Open /api/receipt/<generationId> to see OpenRouter's own record for that answer next to what we billed. Your account page exports every call as CSV.

          
Balance is not a token. It is prepaid AI credit held by GHOSTKEY's server. It cannot be traded or withdrawn.

Errors

          
Errors use the OpenAI shape: {"error": {"message": "…", "code": "…"}}.

          
HTTPCodeMeaning

            
401invalid_api_keyKey missing, wrong or replaced.

            
401key_expiredThe key passed its expiry date. Extend it or make a new one on your account page.

            
402key_daily_limit_reachedThe key spent its daily limit. It works again at 00:00 UTC, or raise the limit.

            
402insufficient_balanceBalance too low for this call. Add balance or lower max_tokens.

            
402model_needs_balanceFree calls work only on low-cost models.

            
400bad_modelUnknown model id, or no zero-retention provider for it right now.

            
400bad_messagesThe messages array is missing or malformed.

            
429slow_downToo many requests. Wait a moment.

            
502upstream_errorThe model provider failed. You are not charged.

          

        


        

          
Privacy

          
Every request goes to OpenRouter with GHOSTKEY's own key, shared by all users. We remove your IP address, device, cookies, wallet address, account ID and the user field.

          
We add provider: {"zdr": true, "data_collection": "deny"} to every request. Zero Data Retention means the provider keeps nothing after answering. OpenRouter publishes the list of ZDR providers.

          
See it for yourself in the inspector, or call POST /api/inspect with your own request.

        


        

          
Ghost Credit

          
Each $GHOST trade on Pons costs 3%: the 1% Pons fee plus a 2% creator tax, locked at launch. The creator tax and 0.7 points of the Pons fee are the creator fee. Each hour we claim it: 25% becomes AI credit for holders, split by average balance. The rest goes to the team. Each hour's split is posted on-chain as a Merkle root.

          
Ghost Credit lands in your GHOSTKEY balance and works for chat and the API. Read how it works and check any wallet.
